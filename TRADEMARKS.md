# MemQL trademarks

The [MemQL trademark policy](docs/public/overview/trademark-policy.md)
explains use of the MemQL name and logo, descriptive references, independent
product branding, and requests for branding permission.

The policy is separate from the unmodified [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE).
It adds no restrictions on using, modifying, distributing, or commercially
hosting the software under that license. See the
[commercial-use FAQ](docs/public/overview/commercial-use.md) for examples.
