# Terms of Service

These default Terms of Service are bundled with the identity service as
a starting point. Cluster operators are expected to replace this
document with one drafted with their own counsel before customer-facing
launch.

Override at runtime by setting `IDENTITY_TOS_OVERRIDE_PATH` to a
filesystem path holding a markdown document with the same front-matter
shape (`documentType`, `version`, `effectiveDate`).

## Acceptance

By using this service you agree to these terms. The version you accepted
is recorded against your user record at the time you signed in. When the
operator publishes a new version, you will be prompted to re-accept on
your next sign-in.
