This notice explains what personal information this installation of
MemQL processes about you, why, who can see it, and what you can do
about it. MemQL is an AI platform built on a time-series memory graph.
The organization that runs this installation, called the operator below,
decides how it is configured and is responsible for your personal
information under the laws that apply to it. The operator's name is
shown on the sign-in page.

If your organization provides your account, your organization decides
what the service is used for and what its users put into it. This notice
adds to your organization's own privacy practices and does not replace
them.

## 1. What the service processes

**Account information.** Your name and email address, and the profile
details you or your organization's administrator add, such as a job
title, phone number, photo, time zone or language, and any other optional
profile fields your organization's administrator fills in.

**Sign-in and security information.** How and when you signed in, the
sessions and devices on your account, and any credentials you register,
stored in protected form. The service emails you when a new sign-in to
your account succeeds.

**Security records.** Records of sign-ins, administrative actions and
similar security events, with the time, network address and browser
information of the request.

**Service logs.** Operational logs that may include your account
identifier, your actions and errors reported by the application.

**Content.** What you and your organization put into the service:
records, files, messages, notes, instructions to AI features, and data
from services your organization connects. This content belongs to your
organization, as the Terms of Service describe, and is processed to
provide the service.

**AI features.** AI features may send the inputs you provide, and
relevant context, to the AI model providers the operator uses, under
terms that do not allow those providers to train on that data. The
service records that a call happened and its result. The operator does
not use your organization's confidential data to train AI models.

**Connected computers and applications.** If your organization connects
its own computers or applications to the service, the service records
what it needs to identify them and the work they do.

**Connected third-party accounts.** If you connect a third-party account,
such as a source-code host or a commerce platform, the service stores
the credential you authorize in protected form and processes the data
that connection delivers, as the connection's own consent screen
describes.

**Abuse prevention.** Where the operator enables them, the service uses
abuse-prevention checks on sign-in and registration.

**Cookies.** The service sets only the cookies it needs to sign you in,
keep you signed in and protect its forms. It sets no advertising or
third-party analytics cookies.

## 2. Why the service processes it

The operator processes this information to provide and operate the
service, to verify that it is you signing in and keep your account
secure, to detect and prevent abuse, to support and troubleshoot the
service, to send you service messages such as sign-in links and security
notices, and to meet its legal obligations. Where the law requires a
legal basis, the operator relies on its agreement with your organization,
its legitimate interest in running a secure service, and the law itself.
The operator does not sell personal information and does not use it for
advertising.

## 3. Who can see it

- The operator's staff who need it to run and support the service.
- Your organization's administrators, who can see your name, email
  address, role, sessions, access to the organization's data, and the
  security records about your account.
- Other users in your organization, to the extent you share content with
  them.
- The service providers the operator uses to run the service, such as
  cloud hosting, email delivery and AI model providers, and the third-party
  services your organization connects. Each processes information under
  its own terms.
- Public authorities, when the law requires it.

## 4. Where it is kept and for how long

The service runs on infrastructure the operator chooses, and the
operator can tell you where.

- **Account.** Kept while your account exists. You can ask for deletion
  from your account settings. After a cooldown period the operator sets
  (30 days by default) your user record is deleted and its personal
  details removed. During the cooldown you can cancel by signing in.
- **Sessions.** A session ends when you sign out, when it expires or when
  it is revoked.
- **Security records.** Records of token refreshes and similar routine
  events are deleted after the period the operator configures (30 days
  by default). Records of sign-ins, administrative actions and other
  security events are kept for the operator's security and compliance
  needs, and keep the email address and the facts of each event even
  after the account is deleted, so that the history stays complete.
- **Service logs.** Kept for the period the operator configures.
- **Content.** Kept while your organization uses the service and handled
  at the end as the Terms of Service describe.
- **Backups.** Copies in backups expire on the backup schedule.

## 5. Your choices

- See and export your account information from your account pages.
- See your active sessions and end one or all of them, and manage your
  passkeys, from your account pages.
- Allow sign-in by passkey only, which turns off sign-in links for your
  account.
- Correct your profile details where the service lets you, or ask your
  organization's administrator.
- Schedule your account for deletion from your account settings, and
  cancel during the cooldown.
- Depending on where you live, you may have further rights, such as
  access, correction, deletion, portability or objection, and the right
  to complain to a supervisory authority. Contact the operator to
  exercise them.

## 6. Security

The service encrypts traffic in transit, stores credentials in protected
form, limits each person's access to the records they are allowed to
see, and logs security-relevant actions. No system is perfectly secure.
If you believe your information has been exposed, tell the operator at
once.

## 7. Children

The service is for use at work and is not directed to children. The
operator does not knowingly collect personal information from a child.

## 8. Changes

The operator may change this notice. The version in force and its
effective date are shown at the top of this page. When a change
materially affects you, the operator gives notice in the platform and by
email to the address on your account at least 30 days before the change
takes effect.

## 9. Contact

Questions about this notice and requests about your personal information
go to the operator. The operator's name is shown on the sign-in page, and
the operator gives your organization its contact details when it provides
the service. If you cannot reach the operator, contact your
organization's administrator.
