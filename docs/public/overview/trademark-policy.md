# MemQL trademark policy

Effective October 10, 2026.

MemQL welcomes independent applications, integrations, distributions, and
hosted services. This policy explains how to identify the technology while
keeping the provider of a product or service clear.

## Software rights and brand rights

ZNAS LLC uses the MemQL name and logo as trademarks identifying the MemQL
project and its official offerings. This policy makes no claim that the marks
are registered.

The software is licensed under the unmodified
[Apache License 2.0](https://github.com/znasllc-io/memql/blob/main/LICENSE).
Section 6 reserves trademark rights, with exceptions for reasonable and
customary descriptions of the software's origin and reproduction of notices.
This policy concerns trademark use only. It does not change copyright or
patent permissions granted by the software license, or add a fee,
notification requirement, or approval requirement for commercial software use.

Nothing in this policy limits fair use, nominative use, or other rights under
applicable law. Permission is not required for uses the law already permits.

## Descriptive references are welcome

You may truthfully describe your relationship to MemQL without asking first,
including in commercial materials. For example:

- "Acme Planner is built using MemQL."
- "Acme Cloud provides independent hosting for MemQL."
- "Acme Connector integrates Shopify with MemQL."
- "Acme Graph is a modified fork of MemQL."

Use MemQL to identify the technology, with your own company or product brand
clearly identifying the provider. Do not suggest endorsement, certification,
or an official relationship that does not exist. Where context could confuse
readers, make the independence explicit, for example: "Acme is an independent
provider and is not affiliated with or endorsed by ZNAS LLC."

Articles, tutorials, comparisons, reviews, and other lawful discussion of
MemQL do not require approval. A reference or criticism need not be favorable.

## Branding that needs permission

Except where permitted by applicable law, obtain written permission from
ZNAS LLC before using the MemQL marks as the brand of your own company,
product, or service, or as an endorsement or certification mark. Examples
include:

- Naming an independent company or service "MemQL" or "MemQL Cloud."
- Using the MemQL logo as your application's icon or your company's logo.
- Marketing yourself as an "official MemQL partner" or "MemQL certified."
- Using a domain name, account name, or presentation that would misleadingly
  suggest your service is operated or endorsed by ZNAS LLC.

This distinction applies to free and paid offerings alike. Charging money,
hosting the software, serving many customers, or running a large company
does not by itself create a branding-permission requirement.

## Distributions, forks, and logos

Redistributing an unmodified MemQL release under its original name, with its
bundled branding and required notices intact, is allowed. Identify yourself
as the distributor or hosting provider; do not present your distribution
channel or service as an official ZNAS LLC offering.

When marketing a modified distribution as your own product, use a distinct
product brand and do not present your modifications as an official MemQL
release. You may still identify its origin with language such as "a fork of
MemQL." Do not remove legal notices that must be preserved under the
applicable licenses.

For optional public acknowledgments, plain-text "Built using MemQL" or
"Powered by MemQL" is welcome. It does not make you a partner or certify your
product. A link to [memql.io](https://memql.io/) is appreciated, not required
by this policy. Do not use or alter the logo to suggest an official badge,
partnership, or endorsement without written permission. Lawful descriptive
uses and rights already granted by applicable licenses remain unaffected.

## Requests and partnerships

For a public branding request, open a
[GitHub issue](https://github.com/znasllc-io/memql/issues/new) describing the
proposed product name, provider, intended use of the marks, and a mockup or
example. The project maintainer is
[@znas-io](https://github.com/znas-io). Request a private contact channel before
sharing confidential business information.

A branding permission or partnership exists only when agreed in writing;
an inquiry or silence is not approval. Any agreement covers its stated
scope and does not reduce the rights already granted by Apache 2.0.

See the [commercial-use FAQ](commercial-use.md) for software licensing,
attribution, hosting, and optional collaboration.
